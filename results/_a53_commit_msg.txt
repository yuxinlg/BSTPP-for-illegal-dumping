THE NUMBER QUESTION, VERIFIED AGAINST THE REGISTER'S TEXT AND REPORTED
WHICHEVER WAY IT FELL. D-42 supports reuse, explicitly: "A config-owned
numeric argument -- any value a config factory validates or stores -- is

sp_var_mu is config-owned (a ModelConfig quantity under the S_WP2 declared at

the coercion D-42 names as the defect. D-42 further anticipates this exact

THE HARM IS NOT CI-9's AND THIS COMMIT DOES NOT BORROW ITS JUSTIFICATION.
CI-9's defect was a value accepted and IGNORED, leaving a misleading
provenance record. CI-10's was a value accepted and ACTED ON opposite to what
it said. This one is different again. exp(sp_var_mu) is a PAIRED GAIN --
decode_spatial_field's own docstring says it "restores the log-amplitude
factored out of the spatial draws during VAE training", so the gain is paired
with those decoder parameters. sp_var_mu=True coerces to 1.0 and applies
exp(1.0) ~ 2.72 where the calibrated default gives exp(2.0) ~ 7.39: the
spatial log-intensity field comes out at the wrong amplitude by a factor of
~2.7, the fit runs, every diagnostic is well formed, and nothing reports a
problem. THAT IS A DIFFERENT MODEL, NOT A MISLABELLED ONE -- and the
distinction matters, because a provenance defect can be repaired after the
fact from the record and this cannot.
  '2.0' IS THE SUBTLER ACCEPT and the reason a TYPE check is required rather
  than a range check. float('2.0') == 2.0, so the string yields the CORRECT
  NUMBER BY COINCIDENCE. No inspection of results can ever catch it, because
  there is nothing wrong with the results -- until someone passes '2,0', or a
  value read from a CSV column, and the coincidence stops holding. A range
  check on the coerced value would pass it forever.

why it is REUSED rather than reimplemented. The incidental TypeError from
