COVERAGE OF THIS CLASSIFICATION (D-39: a gate states its own)
  Decision-clause detection is LATEX-SPECIFIC -- it keys on
  `\textbf{D-nn} &` rows and `\paragraph{D-nn` headings. MARKDOWN
  FILES HAVE NEITHER, so every anchor in docs/*.md and AGENTS.md
  falls to `narrative` BY CONSTRUCTION, not by judgement. The
  live instance is docs/wp_dependency_graph.md's rho_w and
  routed-items cells: those ARE governed by D-48/D-44 and are
  legitimately open (an unfilled rho_w cell is D-48's precondition
  working, not broken), but this instrument is not what
  establishes that -- DEFERRING_ANCHORS is, and it is consulted
  only for anchors already inside a decision clause.
  SO: `OPERATIVE 0` is a claim about the LATEX REGISTER only.
  It is not a claim that every Markdown anchor is harmless.
